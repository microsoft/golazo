# Glossary

| Term                               | Definition                                                                                                  | Link                                 |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Acceptance Criteria                | Observable conditions used to decide whether a ticket delivered the intended outcome.                       | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| Backlog                            | The column for work the team might do but hasn't committed to yet.                                          | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Coach                              | The person who helps the team spot delays, run meetings, and stick to the practices it agreed to.           | [Roles & Responsibilities](roles.md) |
| Column                             | A stage on the board, such as Analyze or Release.                                                           | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Cycle Time                         | The elapsed time between work starting and the ticket reaching Done.                                        | [WIP & Flow](wip-and-flow.md)        |
| Definition of Done (DoD)           | The set of quality and completion conditions that must be true before a ticket is finished.                 | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| Design Doc                         | A short document peers review before coding. It explains the approach, risks, and how to verify the result. | [Design Docs](design-docs.md)        |
| Interrupt                          | Unexpected work that can't wait for the normal queue but doesn't need a Swarm.                              | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| On Call Engineer                   | The person who takes the first look at incidents and other urgent work. Engineers take turns in this role.  | [Roles & Responsibilities](roles.md) |
| Planning Session                   | The periodic meeting where the team shapes, estimates, and prioritizes upcoming work.                       | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| Planning Poker                     | A way to size work where everyone shares their estimate at the same time to avoid influencing one another.  | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| Planned                            | The rail for work the team chose through its usual planning process.                                        | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Rail                               | A horizontal lane used to separate tickets by urgency or type.                                              | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Ready                              | The column holding the next work items that are clear enough and important enough to start.                 | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Retrospective (Retro)              | The recurring meeting where the team reviews outcomes and chooses process improvements.                     | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| Shepherd                           | The person responsible for keeping a ticket moving through shaping, review, and validation.                 | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| SLA (Service Level Agreement)      | The team's agreed upper limit on how long an active ticket should stay open.                                | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| Spike                              | A short research ticket to answer a specific question and decide what to do next.                           | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| Standup                            | A short daily meeting to see what's blocking active tickets and decide how to move them forward.            | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| Starfish Retrospective             | A broader retrospective format using Start, Stop, Keep, More, and Less categories.                          | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| Swarm                              | The highest-priority urgent work, usually involving shared team attention and rapid response.               | [Workflow Overview](task-board.md)   |
| Ticket                             | The smallest piece of work with a useful result someone can review and verify.                              | [Tickets & Sizing](tickets.md)       |
| Value Stream Mapping               | Tracing when a ticket moved and when it waited to see where delays occurred.                                | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
| WIP (Work In Progress)             | The count of active tickets currently in the system, excluding backlog and completed work.                  | [WIP & Flow](wip-and-flow.md)        |
| WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First) | A way to rank tickets by comparing the value of doing them soon with the effort they take.                  | [Meetings](meetings.md)              |
